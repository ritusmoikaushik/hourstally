Privacy

The short version: the site does not collect what you type. Here is the long version.

What happens to your time card

Everything you enter is worked out inside your browser, by code that runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent to a server. There is no account to create and no form that submits.

So that your card is still there if you close the tab and come back, the browser keeps a copy in its own local storage on your device. That copy never leaves the device. Pressing Clear the card deletes the times you entered; your rate, your settings and the name on the card stay, so next week's card is ready to fill in. Clearing your browser data deletes all of it.

Downloads

The Excel file and the print view are generated on your device from what you entered. They are not uploaded anywhere, and we never see them.

Visitor counting

Two services count visits. Neither can see what you type into a calculator.

Google Analytics counts visits and which pages are read, from which country, on what kind of device, and how the visitor found the site. It also records when one of the buttons is pressed (download Excel, print, copy, clear) and on which page. It records only that the button was pressed; the times, rates and names on your card are never sent. It sets cookies so that a return visit is counted as the same visitor. It is loaded from Google's servers.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, Google Analytics sets no cookies until you say yes in the bar at the foot of the page. Until then, and if you say no, Google receives only a count of the page view, with no cookie and nothing that identifies your browser on a later visit. To change your answer, clear this site's data in your browser and the question comes back. Google's own description of what it does with the data is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cloudflare Web Analytics: the site is hosted on Cloudflare, which adds a small script that counts page views and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

Blocking either with an ad blocker or privacy extension breaks nothing on the site.

Advertising

There is none at the moment. If the site earns enough visitors it will carry ordinary display advertising, and this page will say exactly what is loaded, what it can see, and how to opt out, before the first advert appears. Any advertising will follow Google's publisher policies and will never have access to what you type into a calculator.

What is loaded from elsewhere

Only the Google Analytics script, from Google's servers. Fonts, the calculators and images all come from this site's own address. Nothing is fetched from Facebook or any advertising network.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you think the site is doing something this page does not describe, write to hello@hourstally.com.
